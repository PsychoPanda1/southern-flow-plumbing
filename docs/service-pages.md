## Copy boundaries

- Do not add warranties, prices, start dates, response-time promises, emergency availability or equipment-specialist certifications without confirmation.
- FAQs help visitors prepare a conversation; they do not diagnose equipment or replace a project-specific assessment.
- No new licensing, insurance or review-rating claims are added to these pages. Existing homepage credentials still need verification.
- No home address, exact customer address, analytics, form, payment flow or customer database is introduced.
- Native FAQ disclosures and direct call links work without JavaScript; the responsive menu has a no-JavaScript fallback.

1. Ask Chase to approve the service focus, project descriptions, call expectations and asset permissions. Record actual evidence privately, not just a checked box here.
2. Complete the hosting decision from the research roadmap; do not assume a move or provider approval has occurred.
3. Run the full Python/JavaScript checks. The public artifact should contain 42 files in this revision, including the three declared indexable pages and custom 404.
4. Check both pages directly on phones and desktop, keyboard/FAQ operation, enlarged text, mobile menu, cross-page anchors, call actions and image layouts.
5. Following publication approval, verify the successful deployment and exact live content at all three canonical routes. Test a missing route and confirm HTTP status, redirects and root-relative assets on the actual host.
