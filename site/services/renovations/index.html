Planning questions

Before the first fixture is chosen.

What should I have ready when I call?

Share the project address, rooms involved, current stage and preferred timing. Mention any existing plans, fixture selections and general contractor involvement.

Can I discuss an older or historic home?

Historic-home renovation is part of Southern Flow’s featured work. Explain what you hope to change and what should remain; ask Chase to confirm the scope and requirements for your property.

Who handles permits and coordination?

Ask who is responsible for permits, inspections, fixture purchasing and coordination with other trades before accepting a proposal. These responsibilities should be clear for your specific project.

Can this website confirm a start date?

No. Call Chase to discuss current availability, project readiness and the sequence of work. The website does not reserve appointments.
