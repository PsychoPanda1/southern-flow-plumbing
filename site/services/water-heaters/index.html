Before you call

A clearer picture of your project.

Share what you know. You do not need to diagnose the equipment yourself.

  • Your location. Give the neighborhood or project address so Chase can confirm availability.
  • Your current setup. Mention how many water heaters you have and any model information already available to you.
  • What has changed. Describe the hot-water issue or the reason you are planning a replacement.
  • Your timing and access. Explain your preferred schedule and where the equipment is located.

Planning questions

Start with the right conversation.

Can you tell me the replacement price online?

The site does not provide a fixed replacement price. Call to discuss your equipment and project scope, and ask what the estimate includes before authorizing work.

Should I ask about repair or replacement?

Describe the issue and ask Chase what assessment is appropriate. A website cannot determine the condition of your equipment or select the right solution for your home.

Do you work outside Charleston?

Southern Flow’s project journal includes Seabrook Island and other Lowcountry communities. See the featured areas, then call to confirm availability for your address.
